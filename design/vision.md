# Vision

## Settled

- **What it is:** a year alone in the Alaskan wilderness, where calories, cold, fire, hunting and fishing decide whether you last. The Backcountry mod builds its gameplay (`systems.md`); Forge Labs' public packs on Modrinth, [Alaskan Wilderness](https://modrinth.com/modpack/alaskan-wilderness) and [Amazon Rainforest Survival](https://modrinth.com/modpack/amazon-rainforest-survival), are the source of the world and rendering settings.
- **What the player does each hour:** keeps a fire, finds water and food, hunts, traps and fishes, preserves and stores what the season gives, and builds a camp that will carry them through the winter, while the Satellite Messenger tells them what the weather and their body are doing.
- **The frame:** day 1 is 3 May at a drop-off on the coast with a small kit. A survivor whose BMI falls to 17 is extracted; one who dies is dead; either way a new survivor arrives at the drop-off in the same world. Each 3 May the year's summary shows, and play goes on.
- **A survivalist's experience:** what a survivalist alone in Alaska would not meet or do is not in the pack: no caves to explore or ore to dig, no other worlds, no magic, machines or exotic gear (`systems.md` S12, `world.md` W3).
- **Who plays:** one survivor, or friends as one team sharing their stores, in singleplayer or on a server.
- **Version:** Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge.
