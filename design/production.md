# Production

## Settled

- **Release:** private to friends: the repository is public, a Prism instance updates itself from GitHub Pages (`https://roman-16.github.io/backcountry/1/Backcountry.zip`), and the mod jar comes from the GitHub release. A version is its dated section in `CHANGELOG.md`, released when it reaches main.
- **Performance:** the shipped pack draws with Complementary Unbound through Iris and Distant Horizons' far view, 1,024 chunks out. Distant Horizons SeedGen draws the far land from the world's seed, in singleplayer and on a server, which shares the seed with its players; its sampler matches the island the game generates (`/seedrenderer validate 2000 6000` on seed 1: 83% of columns to the block, the middle error 0, 95% within 19 blocks), its chat lines go to the log only and it keeps Distant Horizons' own share of each frame for loading. Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix, ImmediatelyFast, ScalableLux, C2ME, Noisiumed, Entity Culling and More Culling speed up the game, memory, drawing, light and chunks (C2ME and ScalableLux are alpha builds, and `just shape` measures the same island with them); Dynamic FPS draws 10 frames a second while the game is in the background, Packet Fixer gives slow connections longer time-outs, and spark (`/spark`) profiles a lagging server. The frame rate bench (`bench <seconds>` in the dev client, the `playthrough` skill) turns the camera once around: on the user's desktop at 1920 by 1080 and 8 chunks it held 60 frames a second on average and 59 at its lowest once the far land had loaded, 59 and 49 a minute into a fresh world, and 55 and 13 in the world's first seconds (1 at its lowest without SeedGen); a lowest below 30 after the first minute is a finding.
- **Night and the shader:** Iris draws Complementary Unbound from the first launch (`pack/config/iris.properties`). Without the shader More Darkness darkens the night: a moonless night is black beyond the fire, a full moon shows the land in pale grey, and the eyes adapt over a few seconds; the mod has it read the screen's brightness a few frames late instead of waiting on the graphics card every frame. With the shader on, the mod switches More Darkness off and the shader's own night settings (`generator/shader.toml`) give the night. The shader is Complementary Unbound patched at launch by Euphoria Patches, whose added options keep their defaults; its pop-up, in-game messages and update check are off. One moon serves both: the new, partial and full moons' light are `balance.toml` `[sky]`, read by the shader's options and More Darkness alike, and the clouds float at `[sky]` `cloud_height`, 384, above the build limit with and without the shader.
- **Server:** the team plays on an exaroton server. `just exaroton <server>` puts the tree's server pack on it while it is offline: NeoForge, launched by its server starter jar, the mods, the configs and the pack's server properties, uploading only what changed and removing what the pack no longer holds. The world, the whitelist and the operators stay; a running server is refused.

- **Tooling:** game tests run headless with the pack's server mods; the dev client and the dev server are driven by `scripts/client.py` and `scripts/server.py`, with pretend players for a team. The client walks (`walk <x> <z>`) and keeps checkpoints of its world, and `/backcountry playthrough` lives a survivor through game days on the dev server for balance; the `playthrough` skill runs them.
- **Balance** follows the playthroughs: in a camp with a roof, a fire and hide clothing, a Normal ration with 1,024 kcal of work a day keeps a survivor near their weight through a year (77 to 76.1 kg), and a hard year of Lean rations and 2,048 kcal of work a day ends at 66.1 kg, near the reference year's 67 kg.
