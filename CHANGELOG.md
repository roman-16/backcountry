## [Unreleased]

## [3.1.0] - 2026-11-27

### Highlights

- **Sleep anywhere** - At night, sneak and right-click the ground to lie down; the Sleeping Bag and Pad now decide how warm the night is, and a night too cold to survive is refused.
- **Every working block explains itself** - The Cooking Pot, Drying Rack and Woodpile open screens like the campfire's, and sneaking at any working block shows its progress.
- **Tougher bears** - A bear takes 3 arrows to the chest, 2 from the heavy bow, and charges whoever hit it.
- **Fat and food that make sense** - Fat roasts on the fire, the pot renders it in pairs, fish dries outdoors in summer, and food that made you sick says so.

### Added

- Sleep without gear: at night, sneak and right-click the ground with an empty hand to lie down where you stand. The Sleeping Bag keeps you warm while asleep and the Sleeping Pad keeps off the cold ground; when it's too cold to sleep through the night, lying down is refused.
- The Cooking Pot, the Drying Rack and the Woodpile open a screen like the campfire's, and sneaking at any working block shows its progress: pots, the rack, the woodpile, the smoker, the cache, traps and the fish wheel.
- Roasted Fat: Animal Fat roasts on a campfire in 30 seconds, with no parasites.
- Game Guts bait River Fishing's hooks and the Tip-Up, which catches twice as often baited.
- JEI shows each gathering entry's tool, sharpening, copying a map and lighting a fire, each animal's months and where it lives, and an information page for gear whose use isn't a recipe.
- A smoky room shows on the body panel, with how to clear it.
- Food poisoning names the food that caused it, and that food reads "Made you sick before" afterwards; a noted plant that poisons reads "Poisonous".

### Changed

- Bears take 3 arrows to the chest to drop, 2 from the heavy bow, and charge whoever hit them; an axe or a stray arrow only angers them.
- Meat, fish, offal and fat stack to 16.
- A brush with a campfire no longer burns you: only standing in it for a second does.
- Comforts hold spirits at the middle and good events lift them higher, settling back over days; illness wears them down.
- The Sleeping Bag is sewn from 3 cured hides and 2 cordage.
- Drying slows spoiling, so fish dries outdoors in summer, and the rack warns when a piece will spoil first.
- The Cooking Pot cooks a recipe's foods twice or three times over in one go, and says when a mix cooks nothing.
- The Moose Head spoils like raw meat.
- The Food Cache screen counts the days for everyone online and says how to keep raiders off and the food cool.
- Blacking out from exhaustion says why in chat, and a bear's raid says how to keep it off.

### Fixed

- Bear meat counts as meat: it smokes, dries and scents a cache.
- A Camp Pot lifted off the fire keeps how far it boiled.
- JEI's search box no longer covers the body panel.
- Voice chat connects on exaroton.
- Grass no longer drops barley, corn or pea seeds.

## [3.0.0] - 2026-11-27

### Highlights

- **Ten items of your own** - You arrive with the safety gear and choose ten items of eighteen, as on *Alone*; every new survivor chooses again.
- **A homestead for winter** - Sealed rooms hold a fire's heat, tall caches keep raiders out, and drying racks, salt and pemmican store the summer's food; lean meat alone now brings rabbit starvation.
- **Spirits and winter** - Spirits rise and fall with the day's comforts, and winter brings thin ice, numb hands, smothered fires and charging moose.
- **Knowing the island** - The Messenger's Year page shows the seasons ahead and its Notes page what you have found; draw a birch-bark map, blaze your trails, paddle a canoe and set a fish wheel among new animals and a coast full of food.

### Added

- A ten-items screen on arrival: the Satellite Messenger, Bear Spray, Med Kit, Water Bottle, Binoculars, Canvas Pack and Headlamp are issued, and you pick up to ten of 18 items, among them an Axe, a Bow Saw, a Ferro Rod, a Gill Net, a Hunting Bow, Tarps, a Sleeping Pad and Pemmican.
- G opens the worn slots for the back, neck and hands, and the pack, binoculars and mittens show on your body.
- JEI pages for every way the island gives things: gathering, dressing, the pots, the smoker, the drying rack, salting, the birch tap, the woodpile, traps and the fish wheel.
- Sealed rooms hold a fire's heat, and the Warmth page names the room and its air; a fire inside needs a smoke hole straight above it, or the room fills with smoke.
- The Stone Fireplace burns half the wood and the Stone Chimney carries its smoke; a snow shelter keeps you alive on your own heat, and a cellar dug into the ground stays near 4 °C.
- Rabbit starvation from lean meat without fat, with animals fattest in their season; Bone Marrow, Akutaq and Pemmican make up the fat.
- The Drying Rack dries meat and fish to keep for months, faster over a fire; sea water boils down to Salt in the Camp Pot, and salted meat keeps 8 times as long.
- Spirits, with a body line when high or low, a meter on the Body page and an SOS button that glows at the bottom.
- Bears and wolverines raid caches on the ground; a cache on 3 stripped logs is out of their reach and the mice's.
- Moose cows with calves in May and June and bulls in the rut may charge, mostly bluffing; a tree between you stops them.
- New ice is thin and breaks under a heavy load or a sled until it thickens; overflow slush on rivers, and breakup in April with an alert.
- Bare hands go numb in hard cold; Hide and Fur Mittens keep them warm.
- Snow sliding off spruce branches can smother a fire beneath them.
- The Fur Parka, its hood warm under a headlamp, the Fur Hat, and Fox and Wolverine Pelts.
- The Messenger's Year page: the usual temperatures, daylight and wet days for the months around now, and when berries, salmon, bears, the rut, mosquitoes, lake ice, migrations and birch sap come.
- The Messenger's Notes page keeps your field notes of animals watched through the binoculars or taken, fish landed and plants picked; sneaking at something not yet noted names it only "Unknown animal", "Unknown berries" or "Unknown mushroom".
- The Birch-Bark Map draws what you can see when you survey with charcoal in daylight, and copies share the drawing.
- Blaze trees with an axe while sneaking and pile rocks into cairns up to 3 high; a spruce blaze weeps Spruce Pitch for Pitch Tinder that catches in rain, torches, and Pitch Salve that heals a wound or burns within a day.
- Mountain goats, hoary marmots, red and Arctic foxes, coyotes, porcupines whose quills punish a bare hand, robins, sparrows, crows and sperm whales.
- Driftwood on the beaches, already seasoned; clams dug from sand and mussels on stony shores, which can poison, most in summer; goose nests by the lakes in May.
- A bark mushroom held to a fire smoulders for 2 hours and lights a cold fire without the bow drill.
- The Birch-Bark Canoe, faster than the raft, from birch bark, spruce roots and pitch.
- The Fish Wheel catches fish in a stream two blocks deep, up to 4 salmon a day in the run.
- Caribou migrate in big herds in late April and September with an alert, geese come twice as often in May and September, and hares follow a 4-year cycle.
- The Camp Pot is also made from 3 iron ingots and a stick.
- A Homestead chapter of tasks, and Guide topics on fat, spirits, shelter, ice, finding the way and the coast.

### Changed

- Start a new world for 3.0.0: survivors in an older world lose what they wore on the back and at the neck, and its land keeps the old iron ore and gold.
- The arrival kit is the issued gear and your picks; the Camp Pot, Pilot Bread, Sleeping Bag, line and hooks no longer come to everyone.
- Iron is dug, not mined: a shovel in iron-flecked gravel in birch pits and stream beds turns up Raw Iron.
- Light things weigh what they do: a hook 4 g, an arrow about 30 g, a square of Tarp 125 g.
- The moose rut lasts through October.
- Rabbit stew is cooked rabbit and a brown mushroom.
- Item frames and River Fishing's small tackle box and fishing journal take Scraped Hide.
- The first task is "Get an axe", and the kit's Axe counts.

### Removed

- Farming: seeds, crops, bread, hay, beetroot, potatoes, carrots, hoes and the composter.
- Sugar cane, paper, books, bookshelves and the lectern.
- The compass, the clock, vanilla's maps and the cartography table; the Birch-Bark Map replaces them.
- Gold, and leather with its armour.

### Fixed

- The Messenger's Tasks and Guide lists scroll to keep the chosen line in sight on small screens.

## [2.6.0] - 2026-11-26

### Highlights

- **Tracking game** - Big game leaves prints and droppings that lead to it: sneak and look at sign to read whose it is and how old, and stalk moose and caribou into the wind, which Plant Fibre shows.
- **A guide in the Messenger** - The Satellite Messenger's Guide page explains rations, caches, wolves and bears, water, fire, cold and the rest, and messages say why when something goes wrong.
- **Wolves and cold as in Alaska** - Wolves keep their distance unless you hit one, and a cold core is hypothermia, with frostbite only from hard frost.
- **The pack keeps your things** - Items thrown from the pack stay on the ground, and nothing shift-clicked, crafted or smelted into it vanishes.

### Added

- Big game leaves prints in snow, mud, sand and dirt and droppings on any ground; sneaking and looking at sign tells whose it is and how old, and each animal arrives with the trail it came by.
- Moose and caribou smell you from downwind and walk off; sneak and right-click with Plant Fibre to see which way the wind blows.
- A Small Snare on fresh hare prints in snow catches twice as often.
- The Satellite Messenger has a Guide page explaining rations, caches, wolves and bears, water, fire, cold, health, food, hunting and rock.
- Heart, liver, tongue and kidney cook on the campfire, and the Cooking Pot turns a Moose Head into 8 bowls of Moose Head Soup.
- Sneak and right-click pours a Water Bottle or Camp Pot out.
- Sneaking and looking at water names it sea, lake or stream water, and at a carcass tells how long scavengers leave it.
- The Messenger has two new tasks, Track an animal and Cook a stew.

### Changed

- Wolves keep their distance and never hunt survivors; a wolf you hit fights back with its pack.
- A cold core is hypothermia, and only bare skin in hard frost gets frostbite.
- The Camp Pot holds 4 L, and a hotter fire boils it sooner: 8 minutes on Low, 4 on Steady, 2 on Hot.
- Every food carries the water it really holds, so berries and meat count towards the day's water.
- Big game gives its share of meat: a timber wolf 12 pieces, a caribou 19, a black bear 19, a grizzly 48.
- A woodpile holds 16 pieces and fills a block, and full piles stack.
- Rock breaks slowly wherever it sits.
- The headlamp lights only in the dark, so its charge lasts the night.
- A night slept by the fire cooks what lies on it and boils the pot above it.
- Scavengers take an undressed carcass 2 days after it fell.
- Snare Wire is twisted from 2 iron nuggets, and the Hide Poncho takes 5 cured hides.
- Messages say why: an empty ration names where rations eat from, firewood says what seasoning does, raw food warns of parasites, and the Med Kit says what brings hearts back.

### Fixed

- Items thrown from the pack stay on the ground instead of jumping straight back in.
- Shift-clicking onto a stack in the pack keeps every item; crafted items and smelted ingots no longer vanish.
- Eating while too full stops at once instead of chewing on and on.
- Snare Wire and the Hide Poncho can be crafted: each shared its grid with another recipe.

## [2.4.0] - 2026-11-26

### Highlights

- **Walking you can afford** - Walking, running, swimming and climbing cost what the same distance costs a person, so exploring no longer starves a survivor or empties their energy into blackouts.
- **Water you can trust** - Lake water makes you ill 1 time in 4 a litre and stream water 1 in 16, a day after the drink; boiled water is never topped up with lake water, and the Camp Pot shows when it is safe.
- **A fire you can tend** - A fire with nothing cooking on it burns down as it should, and its coals catch the firewood you lay in.

### Added

- Sneaking and looking at the Camp Pot shows how long it has left to boil, or "Boiled water · safe to drink"; looking at a campfire shows its burning time and how long each piece cooking on it has left.
- The Camp Pot shows its water, steams while it heats and rolls at a boil once safe.
- Every condition says in chat as it begins what it does and what cures it.
- The Food page shows how far you walked today beside Move.
- Land a fish has a Bait the hook step, and worms turn up on one dig in 8 on dry days too.
- A swing passes through grass and ferns to hit the animal behind them.

### Changed

- Unboiled water is lake water or stream water, its risk counted per litre and showing a day after the drink.
- A bottle holding boiled water drinks when aimed at a lake or stream, and refuses water from a pot that has not boiled.
- The first day's tasks start with the stone axe; Refill the Water Bottle comes after Boil water and fills from the boiled pot.
- Each ration says in chat what it ate.
- Food of one kind picked within a quarter day stacks.
- A hare gives 800 kcal, a duck 1,000, a goose or loon 3,000, a grouse 280 and a squirrel 240; rabbit stew holds its rabbit's kcal.
- Berry bushes bear as the date stands: on arrival only lingonberry bushes hold a few berries from last autumn.
- "You blacked out" says whether thirst or tiredness did it, and wood too green to catch says to dry it on a woodpile.

### Fixed

- The server no longer crashes when a player leaves, and joining players get the far land's seed again.
- Coals catch queued firewood, and a lit fire burns down, even with nothing cooking on it.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-26

### Highlights

- **Alaska's island** - No caves, ore in the rock, Nether, XP, magic or machines; instead bears that act their season and mostly bluff, sows with cubs, the salmon run, new berries, spruce tea, birch syrup and a sled.
- **Tasks** - The Satellite Messenger opens on Tasks, walking each survivor through their first days, then setting the team's goals for winter and the year.
- **Real work, real calories** - Every activity burns what it would burn a real person, skills make work cheaper with practice, and the Nutrition page shows where the day's kcal went.
- **Company** - Voice chat between teammates within 48 blocks (V opens its menu), and teammates seen typing, sitting and moving with animations.

### Added

- Torches and lanterns burn down: a torch lasts 30 minutes and a lantern an hour, in hand too, with a bar on the slot; charcoal tops up a torch, Rendered Fat a lantern, and a spent lantern hangs dark until refilled.
- A search box in Controls, team marks on mouse button 5, and dark nights without the shader that step aside when it draws.
- The compass is made from iron and a glass pane and points north; the clock is gold and a glass pane; the lead is made from rope, and black dye from charcoal.
- Voice chat between teammates within 48 blocks, open from the first join; V opens its menu.
- Teammates show when they are typing, in a screen or idle, and survivors move, eat and drink with animations, their skins in 3D.
- Sit on slabs and stairs; F5 steps through an over-the-shoulder view, and U swaps the shoulder.
- What you pick up is listed at the top right, the hotbar bounces as items come and go, and sneaking shows the name of what you look at.
- Drag and scroll items between slots, sort a chest, a cache or your pack with a middle click, and keep walking with a screen open.
- Footprints in snow and sand, paths that wear where you walk and grow back, wakes behind the raft, and thunder that arrives after its flash.
- New sounds for screens, items and blocks, music that follows place, time and weather, sparks from fires and lamps, breath in cold air and a growling empty stomach.
- The far land fills in faster, and the game loads, draws and generates faster.
- The Satellite Messenger opens on a Tasks page: each survivor's first days, then the team's goals for winter and the year.
- Fire-making, Woodcutting, Butchering and Archery rise with use, each level making that work quicker and cheaper; the Vitals page shows them, and a new survivor starts over.
- Salmonberries in June and July, highbush cranberries from September into March, and white baneberries that poison.
- Spruce Tea against scurvy, from needles cut off spruce leaves with the knife and boiled in the Camp Pot.
- A Birch Tap gathers sap in April and May that drinks like water and boils down to Birch Syrup.
- Dead spruces in the taiga split into Softwood dry enough to burn from the first day.
- Fish frozen through at -16 °C or colder for a day carries no parasites raw.
- Sows with one or two cubs that charge anyone near them, and bears fishing the rivers from July that leave half-eaten salmon on the bank and defend it.
- The Sled, pulled on a lead, carries 27 stacks or a whole moose, gliding on snow and dragging on bare ground.

### Changed

- The island is a survivalist's: no caves, no ore in the rock but surface iron and gold-flecked gravel in the birch forests, no wild pumpkins, surface lava, honeybees or hazelnuts.
- River Fishing catches only Alaska's fish, with the stick rod in open water and the winter rod through the ice.
- Logs need an axe; the first is the stone axe from loose rocks and twigs.
- The water bar sits where the XP bar was.
- The pack's items and blocks are drawn in vanilla's style: outlines in their own darker colour, light from the top left, and bows, arrows and bottles that keep vanilla's string, feathers and glass.
- The shader is on from the first launch, with the pack's keys and settings: Shift sprints, Ctrl sneaks, FOV 80, GUI scale 3, Bright, full screen.
- The difficulty stays Normal, and F3 shows no coordinates.
- Every activity burns what the same time of it burns a real person, with load, snow, slope, wind and cold counted; snowshoes and a lighter pack save kcal, and the Nutrition page splits the day's burn by cause.
- Rations are larger to match, Lean 1,280, Normal 1,600 and Feast 2,048 kcal a meal, and the stomach holds twice as much.
- Grizzlies come at survivors in spring and autumn and otherwise only when surprised, most charges stop short with a roar unless you run or strike, and black bears flee except at food.
- Mushroom stew takes two brown mushrooms; with a red one it comes out Poisoned.
- Distant Horizons draws 1,024 chunks out, with no warnings in chat.

### Removed

- The Nether can no longer be reached, and dungeons, infested stone and every way to XP, enchanting, potions, redstone machines, swords, shields, metal armour, boats, breeding, taming and bone meal's growth are gone.
- JEI lists only what the island gives: no Nether, End, structure or exotic mod items, no spawn eggs, no Ecliptic Seasons gadgets, no River Fishing electronics, ponds or contracts.
- Advancements are hidden: L is unbound and the pause menu has no Advancements button.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

### Highlights

- **A year counted in calories** - Every survivor's body burns what a real day burns, shown by the energy bolts, the water bar and the conditions beside the hotbar, until extraction at BMI 17 ends the run with its summary.
- **Alaska's own weather** - Days, rain, snow and blizzards follow Anchorage's year, and the Satellite Messenger forecasts them three days ahead and alerts what is coming.
- **Living off the land** - Fires lit with a bow drill, food that spoils unless it is cached or smoked, bows, carcasses and hides, and traps and nets that work while you are away.

### Added

- The body counts every kcal: energy bolts take the hunger bar's place, a water bar sits above the XP bar, and conditions show their names right of the hotbar.
- Every food shows its kcal and fat, and eating stops when the stomach is full.
- The Water Bottle and the Camp Pot fill at any water, and the Camp Pot boils it on a lit campfire.
- Air temperature follows the season, the weather and the hour; shelter, wind, fire, wetness and the Hide Hat, Poncho, Coat and Pants set how cold the survivor gets.
- The weather is the pack's own: days warmer or colder than the season, rain where the air is above freezing and snow where it is not, thunderstorms in summer whose lightning starts no fires, and blizzards that close the view in, as often as Anchorage has them; `/weather` is refused.
- Infected wounds, blisters, frostbite, burns, scurvy, food poisoning, gut infection, parasites and exhaustion, cured with bandages from plant fibre and the Med Kit.
- The inventory is the hotbar alone; a Hide Pack worn on the back opens a row.
- The Satellite Messenger shows the Weather, a three-day Forecast, Warmth, Body, Nutrition and Vitals, and beeps and lights up with alerts for snow, thunderstorms and frost ahead, lost weight and a wasting body.
- At BMI 17 the survivor is extracted; extraction or death shows the run's summary with its weight curve, and a new survivor arrives at the drop-off with the kit, where the Supply Crate replaces a lost Satellite Messenger.
- The year runs 48 days from 3 May with Anchorage's daylight, and each 3 May shows the team's year.
- The Bear Spray drives off what attacks.
- Fire is made and kept: birch bark, tinder, cordage and a bow drill light a campfire that burns a queue of firewood on Low, Steady or Hot, banks coals for a relight, burns faster in the rain and can set a shelter alight when packed too full; a shovel or water puts it out.
- Logs split into softwood and hardwood with an axe; green wood is too green to catch until it has dried on a woodpile.
- The Headlamp arrives in the kit: wound by hand, worn on the head, dimming in deep cold.
- Food spoils by the temperature where it lies, telling how many days it has left here; spoiled food poisons.
- The Food Cache keeps the team's food, shows its kcal, days and scent, and feeds every survivor a Lean, Normal or Feast ration at dawn and dusk; mice raid it at night.
- The Smoker smokes meat and fish over dry firewood for 12 hours to keep them for months, and the Cooking Pot on a campfire cooks stews that also quench thirst.
- Mushrooms are food, blueberries and lingonberries ripen in their season, and crops grow only in the short summer, killed by frost.
- The Satellite Messenger has a Rations page with the next meal and the team's food stores in days.
- The spawn director places every animal out of sight in its habitat and season, fewer in the rain; no monster spawns.
- Hunting with the Knife, the Light and Heavy Hunting Bow and blunt, bone and broadhead arrows: only a hit to the chest kills big game outright, others wound it into a blood trail; the Moose Call works in the rut, and the Messenger alerts when the rut begins and the bears feed.
- Every animal leaves a carcass to carry or dress with the knife into meat, organs, fat, pelts and the moose's head; pelts become scraped and cured hides and rawhide strips for the hide clothing, the Hide Pack and snowshoes for deep snow.
- Grouse, ptarmigan white in winter, loons calling at dusk, and summer mosquitoes by the water that cost sleep.
- Rope, snare wire, wire mesh, stakes and crossbeams build snares, deadfalls, mouse traps that keep mice off caches, gill nets and tip-ups that catch while the survivor is away; crickets and worms for bait.
- River Fishing's catches give kcal by their weight, follow the island's seasons and sky, count in the summary, and late salmon are spawned out.
- A raft of logs and rope that can pitch its rider off in the wind, and lakes that freeze in the cold and thaw in spring.
- Under the shader, a blizzard whites out the view as it closes in without it.
